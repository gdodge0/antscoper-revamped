## Identity corrections

- ACT is Art, Culture & Technology, not a service lot.
- HHCR 1042 resolves to Hewitt Research Hall (HRH), using the UCI ICTS room reference. Original room IDs and schedule codes are retained.
- UEA is Undergraduate Education A; UNEXA remains an alias. Its low volume and façade are approximate.
- Music and Media / AITR / WSH, and Claire Trevor Theatre / Drama share connected exterior geometry in OSM; labels and schedule counts stay separate.
- HICF uses building 523's UCI marker and photograph; the source OSM way is incorrectly named KUCI. The actual radio-station footprint is immediately south.
- Langson's coincident ground/layer-1 outlines are deduplicated; the current layer-1 outline owns its model.
- `uci-barn` and `uci-mc` have independent room overrides and explicit off-map statuses. Neither receives a shared pin or a walking estimate.

All 67 scheduled codes are accounted for: 352 records resolve to mapped teaching locations, and the two UCI records are outside this academic map. Context housing and parking receive simplified volumes and no availability badges.
